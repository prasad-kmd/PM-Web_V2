# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The PM-Web project welcomes contributors and visitors regardless of background, identity, experience, or accessibility needs. Everyone participating in project spaces is expected to help keep them constructive, respectful, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and respectful in issues, pull requests, reviews, and project-related discussions.
- Discuss ideas and technical decisions in good faith; critique code or proposals without attacking people.
- Accept feedback graciously and make room for different perspectives and communication styles.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information, credentials, or security report without consent.
- Follow the repository's contribution, security, and accessibility guidance.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, targeted insults, sexualized attention without consent, doxxing, intimidation, deliberate disruption, and other conduct that a reasonable person would consider unwelcome or unsafe are not acceptable. This also includes encouraging others to engage in such conduct or retaliating against someone who raised a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository, in project issue and pull-request discussions, and in other spaces when someone is representing or participating in the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

To report a conduct concern, email [hello@prasadk.xyz](mailto:hello@prasadk.xyz). Please include what happened, where and when it happened, and any context that would help review the report. Do not post sensitive details publicly. Reports will be handled discreetly to the extent practical.

The project maintainer will review reports and may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action. No fixed response-time guarantee is offered. Retaliation against a person who reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Acknowledgment

This is a project-specific community policy. Questions about its application can be sent to the reporting address above.
